Here is how the next three days will unfold. The schedule is presented as a timeline — mornings are together, afternoons diverge, evenings are for gathering. Each day has its own rhythm.

## The Three Days

<div class="sg-schedule-overview">

**[Day 1: Arrive and Orient](day-1/)** — A lighter day. Morning arrival and orientation, afternoon sessions, and an evening welcome gathering. The day eases in.

**[Day 2: Dig In](day-2/)** — The core day. Morning deep-dives, a working lunch, afternoon workshops, and a long evening gathering. This is where the summit finds its rhythm.

**[Day 3: Close and Depart](day-3/)** — The closing day. Morning synthesis, a final circle, lunch, and a departure window. Lighter, shorter, reflective.
