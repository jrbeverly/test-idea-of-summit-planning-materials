The entrance is the green door on the east side of the building, facing Summit Street. It is unlocked from 8:15 am each day. Here is the path from the pavement to the main hall — four short steps, no wrong turns.

{{< stamp "ARRIVED" >}}

## Step by Step

**1. Approach the green door.** It is the only green door on the block. You will see the summit mark on a small sign beside it. Push — it opens inward.

**2. Step into the entry hall.** It is a small vestibule, warmer than the street, with coat hooks along the right wall and a bench underneath. The welcome desk is ahead on your left, past the inner glass door.

**3. Walk to the welcome desk.** You cannot miss it — it is the first thing you see once you are through the inner door. Someone will be there to greet you. If there is a short queue, the coat room and restrooms are just past the desk to the right.

**4. Take a breath.** After check-in, you are in. The main hall is straight ahead. Coffee is in the kitchen to the left. The [floor plan]({{< ref "/finding-your-way/floor-plan" >}}) has every room marked if you want to orient before the morning circle.
