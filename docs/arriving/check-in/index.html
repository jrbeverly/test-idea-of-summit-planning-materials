Check-in and First Steps

What happens at the welcome desk and where to go next.

The welcome desk is staffed all day, starting at 8:15 am. A welcome host will be there when you arrive. Check-in is quick — a minute or two — and then the space is yours.

At the Desk

Give your name. The host will find your badge, hand you a small printed programme card, and point out the key things nearby: coat room to the right, restrooms just past it, coffee and tea in the kitchen to the left, and the main hall straight ahead.

Your badge has your name and a small schedule summary on the back. Wear it however you like — lanyard, clip, pinned to a bag. It just needs to be visible enough that people can learn your name without asking three times.

After Check-in

You are checked in. The next natural stops, in no required order:

  • Coat room. Drop your things. The bench is for bags, the hooks are for coats. Nothing is locked — the building is ours for the summit.
  • Coffee. The kitchen is to the left of the main hall, past the welcome desk. Coffee, tea, and a light breakfast spread are available each morning from 8:30.
  • Restrooms. Past the coat room on the right. Accessible restrooms are on the ground floor.
  • Find your bearings. The key locations page lists every room and amenity. The floor plan shows where everything is on both levels.

The morning circle begins at 9:15 in the main hall. You will hear people gathering. Until then, settle in, find a coffee, and say hello to whoever is already there.

You have arrived. Coat room and coffee are just past the desk.
