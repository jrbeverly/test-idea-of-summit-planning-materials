Arriving

The moment of arrival: entering, checking in, and your first steps into the space.

You have made it. This section walks you through the door, past the welcome desk, and into your first moments in the office — a small orienting beat before the summit unfolds. Start with the entry sequence to see how the door opens.

§3

Entry Sequence

Step-by-step illustrated guide from the door to the welcome desk.

The entrance is the green door on the east side of the building, facing Summit Street. It is unlocked from 8:15 am each day. Here is the path from the pavement to the main hall — four short steps, no wrong turns.

Step by Step

1. Approach the green door. It is the only green door on the block. You will see the summit mark on a small sign beside it. Push — it opens inward.

2. Step into the entry hall. It is a small vestibule, warmer than the street, with coat hooks along the right wall and a bench underneath. The welcome desk is ahead on your left, past the inner glass door.

3. Walk to the welcome desk. You cannot miss it — it is the first thing you see once you are through the inner door. Someone will be there to greet you. If there is a short queue, the coat room and restrooms are just past the desk to the right.

4. Take a breath. After check-in, you are in. The main hall is straight ahead. Coffee is in the kitchen to the left. The floor plan has every room marked if you want to orient before the morning circle.

The green door is the only one on the block — if you are looking at a different door, you are close but not quite there.

The welcome desk is ahead on your left, past the inner glass door.

Check-in and First Steps

What happens at the welcome desk and where to go next.

The welcome desk is staffed all day, starting at 8:15 am. A welcome host will be there when you arrive. Check-in is quick — a minute or two — and then the space is yours.

At the Desk

Give your name. The host will find your badge, hand you a small printed programme card, and point out the key things nearby: coat room to the right, restrooms just past it, coffee and tea in the kitchen to the left, and the main hall straight ahead.

Your badge has your name and a small schedule summary on the back. Wear it however you like — lanyard, clip, pinned to a bag. It just needs to be visible enough that people can learn your name without asking three times.

After Check-in

You are checked in. The next natural stops, in no required order:

  • Coat room. Drop your things. The bench is for bags, the hooks are for coats. Nothing is locked — the building is ours for the summit.
  • Coffee. The kitchen is to the left of the main hall, past the welcome desk. Coffee, tea, and a light breakfast spread are available each morning from 8:30.
  • Restrooms. Past the coat room on the right. Accessible restrooms are on the ground floor.
  • Find your bearings. The key locations page lists every room and amenity. The floor plan shows where everything is on both levels.

The morning circle begins at 9:15 in the main hall. You will hear people gathering. Until then, settle in, find a coffee, and say hello to whoever is already there.

You have arrived. Coat room and coffee are just past the desk.
