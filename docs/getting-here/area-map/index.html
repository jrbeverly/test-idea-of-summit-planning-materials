The summit is at 42 Summit Street, Downtown District — a two-storey brick building on the east side of the block. It is easy to spot from the street: look for the green door and the wide front windows.

The building is a five-minute walk from Central Station and two blocks from the main bus corridor. Bike parking is at the side of the building, and there is a small car park around back.

Once you are inside, the welcome desk is immediately ahead on your left. The floor plan shows every room and amenity, and the key locations page has a full directory with descriptions.
