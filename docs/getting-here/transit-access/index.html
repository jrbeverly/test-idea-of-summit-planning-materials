Transit, Access and Arrival Times

How to reach the office by train, bus, bike, or car.

The office is easy to reach, however you are travelling. Each option below includes landmark cues and accessibility notes so you know what to expect before you set out.

When to Arrive

Plan to arrive between 8:30 and 9:00 am on the first day. The welcome desk opens at 8:15. Coffee is ready by 8:30. The morning circle begins at 9:15, and you will want time to check in, drop your coat, and find your bearings before then.

If your travel means you will arrive later, that is fine — just slip in quietly. The entry sequence page walks through what happens at the door, whatever time you get there.

By Train

Central Station is the closest stop, a five-minute walk from the office. Come out of the main station exit, turn left onto Market Street, and walk two blocks east. Summit Street is the third right. You will see the brick building midway down the block on your left — look for the green door.

The station has step-free access and lifts to street level.

By Bus

The number 12 and number 24 buses stop on Market Street, one block south of Summit Street. From the bus stop, walk north to the corner, turn right onto Summit Street, and the office is halfway down the block on your left. The walk takes about three minutes.

By Bike

Bicycle parking is at the side of the building, off the small lane that runs along the east edge of the block. Racks are covered and visible from Summit Street. If you are cycling from the city centre, the riverside path is the quietest route — it adds five minutes and is worth it.

By Car

There is a small car park behind the building, accessible from the lane off Summit Street. Spaces are limited — if the car park is full, there is paid street parking on Market Street and a public garage two blocks north. Carpool if you can.

Accessibility

The building has step-free access through the main entrance on Summit Street (the green door). An elevator serves both floors. Accessible restrooms are on the ground floor, past the welcome desk and to the right. If you have questions about access before you travel, contact details are in the support footer at the bottom of every page. Call the venue directly at the number listed there.

If you are taking the train, get off at Central Station — it is a five-minute walk.
