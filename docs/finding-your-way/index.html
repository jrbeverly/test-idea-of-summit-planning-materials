Wayfinding and Practicals

How to read room labels, where restrooms and quiet rooms are, Wi-Fi details, and emergency exits.

Navigating the Space

Every room has a name, not just a number. You will see it on a small sign beside each door, matching the callout numbers on the floor plan a few pages back. If you get turned around, the floor plan (page 17) has the answers — each numbered marker corresponds to an entry in the Key Locations Directory on the facing page.

Room signs use the same icon system as the map, so you can spot a restroom, quiet zone, or coffee station from across the hall without reading a word.

Wi-Fi

The network is SummitGuest. No password, no captive portal, no splash screen. Connect and you are online.

The signal is solid throughout the building. The Main Hall and Kitchen have the strongest connection if you are looking for a reliable spot to settle in.

Finding Your Place to Work

Most sessions happen in the Main Hall or Workshop Room, but you are welcome to work from anywhere during open time.

Main Hall (callout 3) — Tables along the back wall when the room is not in session. Good for quiet work between gatherings.

Workshop Room (callout 4) — Open seating during breaks. Whiteboards are yours. The tables roll, so rearrange as you like.

Quiet Lounge (callout 5) — Armchairs, low light, no conversation. For reading, thinking, or a moment of calm away from the energy.

Kitchen (callout 6) — The social workspace. Good for casual conversations and laptop time between meals.

Restrooms

Two sets, both marked on the floor plan with the restroom icon: one near the Main Entrance (east side), and another past the Kitchen (west side). Both are open all day and refreshed between session blocks.

Quiet Spots and Phone Booths

If you need a call or a moment away from the noise:

Phone booths — Two small booths past the Workshop Room, on your right. Each fits one person and a laptop. First come, first served. The door closes and the fan kicks in — they are properly soundproofed.

Quiet Lounge (callout 5) — No phones, no conversation. Just a calm place to sit.

The bench outside — Past the Main Entrance. Good for a phone call in fresh air. Covered if it rains.

Kitchen and Refreshments

The Kitchen (callout 6) runs all day. Coffee, tea, and light snacks are always out. There is a fridge if you brought lunch, and a microwave. The espresso machine is straightforward — if you are not sure, someone nearby probably is.

Water stations are at the Kitchen counter and in a small alcove beside the Main Hall.

In an Emergency

Two exits, both clearly marked with green signs: the Main Entrance you came in through (east side), and a secondary exit past the Kitchen (west side) that opens to the rear courtyard.

The assembly point is the small park across the street, on the east side. If you need help right now, the Welcome Desk is staffed all day.

Wi-Fi network: SummitGuest — no password.
