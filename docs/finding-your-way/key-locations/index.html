Key Locations Directory

One entry per floor plan callout with name, description, and relevant hours.

1
Main Entrance The green door on the east side. This is where you will come in each morning. Open from 8:00am.
2
Welcome Desk Check in, pick up your badge, and ask questions. Staffed from 8:30am each day. If you need anything, start here.
3
Main Hall The largest room in the building. Opening circle, keynote sessions, and the closing gathering all happen here.
4
Workshop Room Tables that move, walls you can write on, and a setup that changes to fit the session. Past the Main Hall on your right.
5
Quiet Lounge Armchairs, low lighting, and a calm atmosphere. No conversations — this is for reading, thinking, or stepping away from the energy.
6
Kitchen Coffee, tea, and light snacks all day. The espresso machine is good. Fridge and microwave available if you brought your own.
