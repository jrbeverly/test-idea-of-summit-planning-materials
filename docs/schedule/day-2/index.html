Day 2: Dig In

The core day — morning sessions, working lunch, afternoon deep-dives, and an evening gathering.

Day two is the densest day. It stretches from morning coffee to a late evening gathering, and it asks more of you than yesterday did — more thinking, more talking, more making. It is also the day where the summit finds its rhythm.

Pace yourself. The breaks are there for a reason. Not every session needs your full attention. Some of the best moments happen in the gaps.

  1. 8:30
    Coffee and Catch-Up The office is open early. Coffee is ready. Use this time to revisit a conversation from yesterday that you want to continue before the day begins.
  2. 9:00
    Morning Kickoff The host opens the day with a short framing: what today is about, what is different from yesterday, and what to pay attention to. Five minutes. Then we move.
  3. 9:15
    Deep-Dive Sessions Two parallel sessions running in the main hall and workshop room. These are the longer, meatier sessions — ninety minutes each, designed for real exploration rather than survey. Each session has a facilitator, a short opening prompt, and a lot of time for the room to think together. You picked your track at breakfast.
  4. 10:45
    Morning Break Pastries, fruit, coffee, and the inevitable clustering of people who just spent ninety minutes thinking together and have more to say. The break runs a full half-hour — long enough for a real conversation, not just a refill.
  5. 11:15
    Parallel Tracks Continue The second round of deep-dive sessions. Same format, different topics. If you were in the main hall for the first block, consider switching to the workshop room for a change of setting and perspective. Or stay — the deep-dive is designed to work as a two-part arc.
  6. 12:30
    Working Lunch Lunch is served in the kitchen, but eating is not the only agenda. Each table has a question on a card — a conversation starter that connects to the morning sessions. Talk if you want. Eat in silence if you need to. The questions are an invitation, not a requirement.
  7. 14:00
    Afternoon Workshops The afternoon shifts from discussion to making. Workshops are hands-on: write, sketch, prototype, plan. Each workshop has a concrete output — a doc, a diagram, a decision, a thing you can point to. Facilitators provide just enough structure. The room provides the rest.
  8. 15:30
    Afternoon Break Coffee, a stretch, and a moment to check in with yourself. The quiet lounge is there if you need it. You have been thinking hard — a short reset before the final session makes a difference.
  9. 16:00
    Show and Tell We gather in the main hall. Each workshop group shares what they made or discovered — not a formal presentation, more like showing a friend something you just built. Five minutes per group, light facilitation, genuine curiosity from the room.
  10. 17:30
    Free Time A longer gap before the evening. Rest, walk, call home, or find a quiet corner to process the day. The neighbourhood has good food if you want to step out (section 8 has recommendations).
  11. 19:00
    Evening Gathering A longer, looser evening than yesterday. Dinner is served. Someone might bring out a guitar. Someone else might start a fire in the courtyard if the weather holds. Stay late or leave early — the gathering is here if you want it, and no one is counting heads.
