Day 3: Close and Depart

The closing day — morning wrap-up, final sessions, and departure.

Day three is the shortest day and the lightest. The morning holds the final sessions and the closing gathering. The afternoon is for departing at your own pace.

If you feel a little sad as the summit ends — that is normal. These three days are designed to feel like a small world. Leaving a small world takes a moment. Take yours.

  1. 9:00
    Coffee and Conversation A later start — you have earned it. Coffee is on. The room is quieter than yesterday. Conversations are reflective. People are starting to process what has happened over the past two days.
  2. 9:30
    Morning Circle We gather one last time in the main hall. The host frames the closing day: what we are here to do, what closure looks like, and how to carry what we have built beyond these three days. Short, warm, forward-looking.
  3. 10:00
    Final Sessions The last round of sessions. These are shorter than yesterday — sixty minutes — and focused on synthesis: what did we learn, what do we want to keep doing, what is the smallest next step. The output is not a deliverable. It is clarity.
  4. 11:00
    Morning Break Pastries, coffee, and the beginning of goodbyes. Some people will leave after the closing gathering. Others will stay into the afternoon. This break is a good moment to exchange contact details before the room starts to thin.
  5. 11:30
    Closing Gathering We come together in the main hall for the final time. The host invites anyone who wants to speak to share: a reflection, a thank-you, a commitment, a question to carry forward. No pressure. Some of the best closings are mostly silence and nodding. Then: a few words from the host, an invitation to stay in touch, and the official close of the summit.
  6. 12:30
    Lunch and Goodbyes One last meal together. The kitchen has food. People drift in and out. Some leave directly from lunch. Others stay for the afternoon to help tidy up or continue a conversation that needs more time. There is no formal end. You leave when you are ready.
  7. 14:00
    Departure Window The office stays open until 4pm. If you have a later train or flight, you are welcome to stay — the quiet lounge is open, the Wi-Fi is on, the coffee is still hot. Lost something? Check the lost-and-found by the welcome desk. Section 9 has notes on what comes next.
