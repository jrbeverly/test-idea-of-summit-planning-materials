Day 1: Arrive and Orient

A lighter first day focused on arrival, orientation, and settling in.

The first day is paced for arrival. You are not expected to be at full energy by 9am. The morning eases in. The afternoon gives you options. The evening is for gathering without agenda.

If you arrive feeling tired from travel, that is expected. The day is designed so you can participate fully without having to perform. Show up. Listen. Join when you want to.

  1. 8:30
    Doors Open The main hall is open and the coffee is on. Arrive, drop your bag in the coat room, and settle in. There is no rush — this first half-hour is for arriving at your own pace.
  2. 9:00
    Welcome Circle We gather in the main hall. Our host opens the summit with a short welcome, sets the tone for the next three days, and invites everyone to introduce themselves — name, where you came from, and one thing you are curious about.
  3. 9:45
    Getting to Know Each Other A light, structured activity designed to break the ice without making anyone cringe. You will be in small groups of four or five. No trust falls, no role-playing. Just good questions and a reason to talk to people you have not met yet.
  4. 10:45
    Morning Break Stretch, refill your coffee, step outside for air. The kitchen has pastries and fruit if you are hungry. The quiet lounge is open if you need a moment away from the group.
  5. 11:15
    How These Three Days Work A short walkthrough of the summit rhythm: how sessions run, where things are, what is optional and what is together. You will learn how to read the schedule (you are looking at it), how to find your way (section 4 has you covered), and who to ask if you need anything (section 6).
  6. 12:30
    Lunch Catered lunch in the kitchen and main hall. Seating is family-style — grab a plate and sit next to someone you have not talked to yet. The food is good. Dietary needs noted during registration have been accounted for.
  7. 14:00
    Afternoon Sessions Two parallel sessions in the main hall and workshop room. Topics will be posted on the board by the kitchen the morning of. Sessions are conversational, not lecture-style — the facilitator opens with a short frame, then the room talks. You are welcome to switch between sessions.
  8. 15:30
    Afternoon Break Coffee, tea, and a chance to process the afternoon before we gather again. The kitchen tends to be where the best hallway conversations happen.
  9. 16:00
    Closing Circle We come back together in the main hall to reflect on the day. The host invites a few voices to share: something that surprised you, a conversation you are still thinking about, a question you are carrying into tomorrow. This is optional to speak, good to witness.
  10. 17:00
    Free Time A gap to rest, walk, or explore the neighbourhood (see section 8 for local tips). The office stays open. The quiet lounge is always available.
  11. 18:30
    Welcome Gathering An informal evening at the office. Drinks, light food, music, and no agenda. Bring yourself. Stay as long as you like. This is the first of two evening gatherings — low-key, high-warmth, entirely what you make of it.
